# Contract Invariants

- Each burner price-note nonce is single-use for its caller ([burner nonce test](../contracts/test/OakBurner.t.sol#L493)).
- Every burner note is bound to `msg.sender` (`if (n.wallet != boundWallet) revert NoteWrongWallet()`), is within 300 seconds, and is within 10% of live OAK/ETH spot before any swap, and its output floor uses the fee frozen at OAK's launch and copied into `launches(poolId)` word 10 at graduation, plus the immutable creator tax and a 150 basis-point impact allowance. The floor stays between 9000 and 9607 basis points; changing the hook's global default for future token launches cannot change it.
- Each `buyAndBurn(usdgIn, note, sig)` call on `OakBurner` (`0x660b86c5a3f9E1278cB5F01763fd0a630221C18B`) spends at most `oracle.liquidityUsdgE6() / 200` (`0.5%` of live pool USDG liquidity) from USDG already held by `OakBurner` and sends all bought `$OAK` to `0x000000000000000000000000000000000000dEaD`.
- A burn disarms an armed sweep only when it spends at least `min(ceil(armedBalance / 100), acceptedChunkCap)`, so one permitted maximum chunk can always disarm a working burner. A completed sweep transfers no more than the balance recorded when armed; later deposits remain for a new timer ([armed-balance tests](../contracts/test/OakBurner.t.sol)).
- Oracle construction and deployment require the OAK launch record to exist and match the native pair, fee 0, tick spacing 200, and pinned current Pons hook.
- `OakTickMath.sqrtRatioAtTick` equals the test-only Uniswap v3 reference and is strictly increasing across adjacent valid ticks; deterministic bounds, bit-transition and 1,000-tick-stride checks, a 100,000-run fuzz profile, and an opt-in exhaustive sweep cover the claim ([differential test](../contracts/test/OakTickMathDifferential.t.sol)).
- Legacy v1 and v2 billing contracts (`0xF1C5d40997e1151B125a41e822C4278b153c4610` and `0x5e0d93D4bad53388839Ac5A53dB716b70d8606A8`) remain deployed on Robinhood Chain and are unused; Oak is free to use.
